# Public full-loop search recheck (2026-10-09)

The recheck covered official Zenodo measurement records, DOI/publisher pages, public GitHub pressure-flow repositories, DOE/NREL/NLR fueling and safety resources, and public station-infrastructure portals. No new public dataset met the frozen station-to-vehicle eligibility rule.

The MetHyTrucks Group A and Group B records provide useful temperature/pressure measurements for sampling-system component diagnostics, but they do not provide a synchronized vehicle receiving-tank and station-controller holdout. The HSR-Rig repository is a metal-hydride storage-reaction laboratory rig, not a refueling station. The Cal State LA multi-year HRS paper is a high-value custodian lead with aggregate operating evidence, but its raw synchronized logger archive was not publicly downloadable.

The official NREL H2IQ heavy-duty simulator report provides useful internal-tank pressure and mass-flow figures, while H2FillS and HDTADA document modeling and dispenser-test workflows. The NLR H2SAFE archive is useful for controlled-release and detector-response checks, and NREL/California/European portals provide station-level aggregate or inventory context. None of these public artifacts publishes a synchronized measured station/dispenser pressure, vehicle pressure/temperature, transferred mass, common time base and reuse terms as one independent raw cohort.

The search also identified four useful published experiment leads: a direct-pressure HRS benchmark validated against SAE J2601 laboratory data (DOI `10.1016/j.renene.2024.120410`), NREL/Powertech Type-IV fueling temperature experiments (DOI `10.1002/ente.202300239`), a high-aspect-ratio Type-IV tank experiment at an Empa HRS (DOI `10.1016/j.ijhydene.2025.152513`), and a multi-year real-station operation study (DOI `10.1016/j.ijhydene.2023.04.084`). These are valuable pressure/temperature/APRR/operational benchmarks and custodian leads, but the raw synchronized station-to-vehicle logger files were not retrieved, so they remain outside the independent full-loop holdout.

Two additional local-search leads were recorded. The UCI early-years HRS study (DOI `10.1016/j.ijhydene.2020.08.251`) describes fueling time, dispensed amount and hose/storage pressure-temperature fields, but no downloadable synchronized logger archive was found. A public CSIC/Datos.gob supplement provides one-year on-site HRS operational-strategy and finite-state-machine outputs under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; it is a simulation supplement rather than measured full-loop data. Both leads improve schema and operating-strategy grounding but do not close the independent validation gate.

The full-loop gate therefore remains open. This recheck is a discovery/exclusion record, not validation evidence. The local private collection remains materially richer for station-side calibration, but its vehicle-side semantics are not independently attested.
