# ELVHYS public consequence dataset

The ELVHYS 4.2 record on DataverseNO is a useful public auxiliary source for consequence-model development:

- DOI: [10.18710/JXJP0H](https://doi.org/10.18710/JXJP0H)
- License: CC0 1.0
- Scope: 48 cryogenic-hydrogen transfer-connection-space experiments and 198 files covering dispersion, pressure peaking and deliberate ignition.
- Facility: a 1 m3 connection space at the HSE Science & Research Centre, Buxton, UK.

The record is **not** promoted to the independent H70 station-to-vehicle full-loop holdout. It does not provide a gaseous H70 fill trace with synchronized vehicle/receptacle pressure, temperature, transferred mass, station controller state and protocol metadata. It therefore cannot support a claim that the complete digital twin has been externally validated, and it does not permit the IJHE readiness goal to be closed.

If the model is extended to cryogenic transfer spaces, freeze the selected files and their hashes before reading outcomes, declare the domain-transfer assumptions, and score dispersion/pressure-peaking/ignition endpoints without fitting on the selected cases.
