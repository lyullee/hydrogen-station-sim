## Frozen decision boundary

The current apparatus-resolved release model and evaluator are locked by the
SHA-256 hashes in
[`cheng_2026_h2co2_discharge_preaccess_protocol_2026_10_13.json`](cheng_2026_h2co2_discharge_preaccess_protocol_2026_10_13.json).
The supplement can become a numerical holdout only if it provides reusable,
synchronized pressure, temperature and flow or released-mass series, complete
apparatus and valve metadata, at least four cases over two pressure groups,
and usable rights.

The current production release model is a pure-H2 model. The reported
H2/CO2 mixture cannot be represented as pure hydrogen. Unless mixture
thermophysical properties are implemented and frozen before numerical outcome
access, the new apparatus material is limited to metadata or development
evidence. Pure-H2 curves copied from already consumed literature are not an
independent holdout.

No current validation gate changes merely because the article or supplement
exists.
