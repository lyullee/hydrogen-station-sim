# Request for institutional ethics determination

Study: **Blinded expert evaluation of hydrogen-station decision-support text**

Protocol: **HIAD-SAGA-2601**

Current status: **No reviewers recruited and no ratings collected**

## Decision requested from the institution

Please determine whether this activity requires formal human-participant review,
qualifies for exemption, or is not considered human-participant research under
the institution's applicable policy and jurisdiction. The research team will not
make that determination itself and will record the institutional decision and
identifier before recruitment or rating.

## Purpose and procedures

The study compares masked written responses to 24 public historical hydrogen-
refuelling-station incident vignettes. Qualified professionals independently
score each response using seven fixed 1–5 criteria and two binary safety fields.
They do not operate equipment, encounter hazardous material, receive simulated
personal risk or make decisions affecting a live facility. Estimated completion
time should be established during the nine-case development pilot and disclosed
before holdout recruitment.

## Participants

Three adult professionals with relevant hydrogen/process-safety, HAZOP,
emergency-response or HRS operating experience will be recruited. Eligibility
requires a relevant role plus either three years of experience or a documented
relevant qualification. Subordinates should not be recruited by a direct manager
where declining could reasonably affect employment, evaluation or access to
professional opportunities.

## Risks and safeguards

Foreseeable burdens are time, fatigue and possible professional discomfort when
judging safety advice. Reviewers may pause or withdraw before database lock. No
live emergency, deception, clinical procedure or sensitive personal question is
involved. Responses are masked; reviewers do not receive another person's scores.
Identity/contact information is stored outside the research repository under the
institution's approved access controls.

## Data collected

- coded reviewer ID;
- professional role and relevant years of experience;
- relevant qualifications and experience categories;
- prior familiarity with the evaluated system;
- conflict-of-interest and independence declarations;
- ordinal scores, binary safety marks and free-text rationale; and
- completion time/date where institutionally permitted.

No name, personal email, telephone number, employer identifier or signature is
placed in the public repository. Reports use aggregate reviewer characteristics.
The identity-code key, if required, is held separately by the responsible
institutional investigator.

## Consent or information process

Before participation, reviewers receive the accompanying information sheet,
institutional contact, purpose, task, expected duration, data handling, voluntary
participation statement, withdrawal limit, foreseeable burden and publication
plan. The institution should specify whether documented consent, an information-
sheet acknowledgement or another process is required.

## Compensation and conflicts

Compensation, if any, must be set before recruitment and must not depend on the
direction of ratings. Study developers, vignette coordinators and anyone with a
material undisclosed conflict may not serve as blinded response reviewers.

## Publication and data sharing

The intended output is a peer-reviewed validation article. Public release may
include masked model responses, coded ratings and aggregate reviewer descriptors
only to the extent permitted by the institutional determination and participant
information. The identity key and direct contact data will never be deposited.
