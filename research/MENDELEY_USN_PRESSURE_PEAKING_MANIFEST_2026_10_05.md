# Mendeley USN pressure-peaking manifest recheck

The public Mendeley record [10.17632/pmk59x4hvc.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/pmk59x4hvc.1)
is a CC BY 4.0 mirror of the University of South-Eastern Norway large-scale
unignited pressure-peaking campaign. The public manifest contains pressure
transducer traces and Coriolis mass-flow channels for all ten eligible cases
(cases 2–11), together with the data description file. The channel file
digests and case/folder identities are recorded in
`mendeley_usn_pressure_peaking_manifest_2026_10_05.json`.

The record itself does not carry the full vent-geometry and initial-condition
mapping in its manifest. Those boundary conditions must be taken from the
associated open paper, [10.1016/j.ijhydene.2020.08.221](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2020.08.221),
and frozen before any new numerical comparison. This is therefore eligible as
an independently traceable consequence-component archive, but it is not an
H70 station-to-vehicle fueling loop and cannot close the full-loop or SAGA
effectiveness gates.

The raw Mendeley files remain outside version control. The recheck only stores
public metadata, file identities, and the claim boundary; it does not copy or
redistribute third-party files.
