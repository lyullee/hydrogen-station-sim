# FCH2RAIL/DLR public trace availability recheck (2026-10-09)

The official FCH2RAIL and DLR records were rechecked as a high-value lead for an independent hydrogen-refueling validation cohort. The DLR publication record describes analysis of measurements from the station and vehicle, while the FCH2RAIL results page exposes the associated papers and deliverables. The public D6.1 report is useful for operating-range and protocol context.

The official pages inspected expose publication/report PDFs and metadata only. No downloadable synchronized station-to-vehicle logger archive, CSV/XLSX/ZIP trace bundle, channel dictionary, controller-state stream or raw-data reuse terms were found. The result is therefore recorded as **official measurement evidence with no public machine-readable trace**.
