# RHeaDHy raw logger request draft

Subject: Request for de-identified RHeaDHy Mid Flow Twin refuelling logger data for independent validation

I am preparing an independent validation study of a hydrogen-refuelling-station safety digital twin and decision-support system. The public RHeaDHy materials document a real heavy-duty Mid Flow Twin campaign at Champagnier, including 18 refuelling tests over six days. I would like to request a de-identified machine-readable export for prospective validation.

Please provide, where available, a common timestamp and the vehicle/receptacle, station and dispenser pressures; vehicle and delivered-gas temperatures; mass flow or transferred mass; initial conditions, tank capacity and SOC; ISO 19885-3 protocol and pressure-ramp metadata; storage-bank selection, valve, compressor and precooler states; stop/abort/fault markers; and calibration, uncertainty and quality flags.

The minimum useful release is eight complete or protocol-defined eligible fills, with a channel dictionary and enough provenance to freeze an untouched holdout before model inspection. Please also state whether the files and derived validation tables may be deposited in an open repository and used in a journal article.

This draft is a planning artifact. It has not been sent and does not imply that the public campaign summary itself is a validation result.
