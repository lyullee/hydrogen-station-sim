# Local data deep scan (2026-10-09)

The local corpus is not small. The measured station bundle alone contains 33 CSV files, about 4.75 GiB of source material and 56,854,143 deduplicated rows. It contains pressure and temperature cycles, cascade/recharge sequences, flow-like and totalizer-like channels, and compressor/valve/ESD/lifecycle state families. That is enough for station-side calibration, chronological holdouts and virtual response training.

An additional privacy-bounded scan covered the other local project roots and two quarantined broad-keyword candidate inventories. The inventories listed 394 entries, representing 206 unique candidate assets after 188 duplicate listings were removed. The candidates fall into four classes: 187 processed protocol/component or case-metric outputs, 7 liquid-hydrogen release/consequence source tables, 11 HAZOP/incident/operational catalogs, and 1 inventory catalog. These are useful supporting material, but none is an independently attested synchronized station-to-vehicle raw cohort.

The broad inventory is a discovery aid, not proof of a full-loop dataset. A file can contain words such as `vehicle`, `station`, `pressure`, `temperature` or `flow` while still being a derived trace, a tank-boundary component experiment, a qualitative catalog, or a simulator export. Those files remain excluded from prospective full-loop validation until their units, channel roles, common time base, initial conditions, controller/protocol boundary and reuse rights are attested.

The local search therefore changes the conclusion from “not enough data” to “substantial station-side data, with the vehicle/dispenser boundary still unverified.” The full-loop validation gate remains open. A privacy-bounded custodian request is already prepared for the missing semantic and rights confirmation. See `local_data_deep_scan_2026_10_09.json` for aggregate counts and the exact claim boundary; no source path, filename, identifier, site/company/manufacturer detail, calendar date or raw row is published.
