# Local user-workspace semantic recheck (2026-10-09)

The search was expanded beyond the project repositories to the user's Desktop, Documents and Downloads data roots. It found 33,491 machine-readable files, 3,935 path-level keyword candidates and 728 candidates that could be screened from headers. Header keywords alone identified 160 files with time, pressure, temperature, flow/mass and vehicle-like terms.

The apparent increase is mostly a naming effect. After provenance classification, 158 candidates were already processed, reference or simulator material inside the known research workspace. Two additional workspace exports contain two vehicle pressure channels, two vehicle temperature channels, two nozzle-flow channels and a total-leak-flow field. They contain 337 and 339 rows, run for roughly 67 seconds, and report zero leak flow throughout. Their schema matches the local dual-vehicle simulator export, so they are retained as regression evidence only.

No new measured station-to-vehicle cohort was found. The scan therefore confirms the earlier conclusion: station-side measured data and public component/accident data are abundant, while a synchronized, independently attested station/dispenser/vehicle logger cohort is still missing. The machine-readable record stores aggregate counts only; no path, filename, identifier, date, site, manufacturer or raw row is published.
