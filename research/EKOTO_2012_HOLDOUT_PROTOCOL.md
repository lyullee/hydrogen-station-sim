# Ekoto et al. (2012) mass-flow holdout protocol

This experiment-level transfer test was frozen before the numerical Figure 3
mass-flow coordinates were opened. The public apparatus inputs are 13.45 MPa,
297 K, 3.63 L, a 3.56 mm restriction and `Cd = 0.75`. The locked model is the
existing HEOS well-mixed adiabatic vessel balance.

At least 15 experimental coordinates are required. A validation claim requires
all three screens: peak-normalized NRMSE <=15%, median absolute percentage error
<=20% over the main trace, and half-peak-time error <=20%. Failures remain in
the record and cannot be tuned away.

The source is Ekoto et al., *Experimental investigation of hydrogen release and
ignition from fuel cell powered forklifts in enclosed spaces*, International
Journal of Hydrogen Energy 37 (2012) 17446-17456,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2012.03.161. The digitization comes from
HyRAM+ 6.1 commit `b45abf9a6d995951311be6aad836f1874e4d420b` under GPL-3.0.
