# Confidential station-boundary calibration summary

The controlled pressure replay uses an owner-approved, de-identified pressure
logger summary. The selected window contains 2,000 sampled points over about
33 hours, with a 60-second median sample period and no reported quality
warnings.

The observed station-boundary pressure range is approximately 52.0–63.9 MPa.
The pressure-derived recharge restart margin is 0.25 MPa and the 95th
percentile positive pressure ramp is about 26.2 kPa/s. These values are
runtime calibration evidence for the station-side replay and are not universal
design limits.

The summary is intentionally separate from vehicle-side validation. It does
not include raw rows, exact dates, site/operator identity, source tag names,
vehicle pressure, SOC, nozzle temperature, or the complete dispenser protocol.
