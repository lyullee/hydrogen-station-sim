# Local cross-station bundle recheck (privacy-bounded)

This record is a de-identified recheck of two owner-controlled station-side
CSV bundles found in the local workspace. It is intentionally aggregate-only:
source paths, site/company names, exact dates, manufacturers, channel names,
values, hashes, and raw rows are not retained.

## What was confirmed

- Two distinct station-side bundles were screened, containing 25 and 8 CSV
  files respectively.
- All screened files had a parseable timestamp sample and a monotonic time
  direction. A reverse-ordered source is accepted as a valid time axis; the
  check does not rewrite or publish the source dates.
- Header-only qualitative screening found pressure and control/state families in
  both bundles. Temperature-family candidates are present in both bundles;
  flow/mass-family candidates are present in the first bundle only. These are
  candidate families, not unit or engineering-semantic attestations.
- The aggregate row counts were rechecked from the local source. The values are
  retained in the JSON artifact only to support reproducibility of the bounded
  inventory and do not identify a site.

## How to use this result

`station_side_transfer_candidate=true` means the two bundles are suitable for a
future custodian-approved station-side transfer or robustness experiment using
the same privacy-bounded contract. It does not activate a new runtime profile
and does not alter model parameters.

The result is not evidence of vehicle-side accuracy, a synchronized dispenser
full loop, hydrogen release consequence accuracy, field safety distance,
failure probability, or certification. Flow units and vehicle-side semantics
remain unconfirmed and require a custodian attestation before any quantitative
use. The existing public and confidential validation gates therefore remain
open where those claims are required.
