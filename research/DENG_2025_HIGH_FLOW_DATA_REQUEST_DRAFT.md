# Data request: Deng et al. 2025 high-flow hydrogen refueling experiment

This draft is a request template only. It does not authorize contact and is not
evidence that the data are available.

## Reference

- Article: *Experimental–numerical optimization of inlet angles for controlling temperature rise during high-flow hydrogen refueling of large-scale storage tanks*
- DOI: `10.1016/j.ijhydene.2025.151093`
- Public record: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2025.151093>

## Requested material

Please provide, if permitted, a de-identified archive of the experimental runs
used for the 35 MPa high-flow refueling study:

1. synchronized timestamps and sampling rate;
2. inlet mass-flow, upstream/downstream pressure, gas temperature and all
   thermocouple/wall-temperature channels;
3. tank volume, aspect ratio, inlet/nozzle geometry and boundary conditions;
4. calibration certificates, uncertainty estimates, filtering and missing-value
   handling;
5. pre-cooling and valve/controller states for each run;
6. licence or written permission for derived-data publication and redistribution;
7. a stable archive identifier and SHA-256 digest.

The intended use is a pre-registered, no-fitting tank-thermal validation of an
HRS digital-twin submodel. The archive would not be mixed with the existing
Powertech/H2Protocol cases, and the protocol would be frozen before reading
evaluation outcomes.

## Claim boundary

The paper is currently a data-request lead only. Its published figures and
reported errors cannot be treated as an untouched raw holdout, and this request
does not change the repository's open full-loop or IJHE readiness gates.
