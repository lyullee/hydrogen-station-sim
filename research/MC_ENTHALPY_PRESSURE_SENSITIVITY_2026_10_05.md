# MC inlet enthalpy pressure-basis sensitivity

The MC Default workbooks expose a vehicle pressure and two source-pressure
channels, but do not expose the pressure immediately upstream of the nozzle.
This post-outcome diagnostic holds measured mass flow and inlet temperature
constant and changes only the pressure used to evaluate inlet hydrogen
enthalpy.

The comparison is a boundary-identifiability check. It does not choose a
production boundary, fit a parameter, validate the station loop, or close an
IJHE/full-objective gate. A future prospective protocol must acquire the
nozzle-upstream pressure (or an equivalent calibrated pressure/enthalpy
boundary) before this ambiguity can be resolved.
