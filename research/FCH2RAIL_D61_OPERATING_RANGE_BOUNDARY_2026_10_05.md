# FCH2Rail D6.1 operating-range boundary (2026-10-05)

The public DLR repository report for FCH2Rail D6.1 describes real refuelling
tests of a transportable HRS and demonstrator train at Zaragoza, El Goloso and
Vigo. The reported setup used 300 bar tube-trailer storage, no chiller and a
10 m hose. Two independent 80 kg, 350 bar vehicle modules were filled in
sequence; each module used sixteen 205 L Type-III cylinders.

The report states that flow was recorded at 5 or 10 s intervals. Reported
average flow rates span 11.54–19.44 g/s, with an example first/second
dispensing pair of 18.34/13.87 g/s, and an average refuelling speed of
0.69–1.17 kg/min. These values are retained as an independent operating-range
and trailer-supply face-validity boundary, not as a reconstructed time series.
