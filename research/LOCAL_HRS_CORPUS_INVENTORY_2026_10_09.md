# Local HRS corpus inventory (2026-10-09)

The local corpus is not small. It contains both a large owner-controlled station telemetry bundle and a separate public validation corpus. They are kept as different evidence classes so a component experiment is not presented as a complete station-to-vehicle validation.

## What is present

- **Measured station telemetry:** 33 files, about 4.749 GiB, 59,272,300 physical rows and 56,854,143 rows after duplicate exclusion. The bundle supports medium/high storage pressure, cascade and recharge behavior, plus temperature-like, flow-like, compressor, valve/ESD, lifecycle and totalizer-like signal families.
- **Public machine-readable material:** 255 locally cached machine-readable files totaling about 3.509 GB (decimal). This includes three unique tank/refueling experiment workbooks with 9,480 sample rows, a partial station/hose reference, endpoint-style dispenser tables, and release/fire/dispersion/detector/ventilation material.
- **Derived simulator exports:** two CSV traces with 337 and 339 rows. They contain vehicle/dispenser-shaped fields, but are generated by the simulator and remain regression fixtures only.

## What can be validated now

The measured station bundle can support chronological station-side checks for pressure cycles, cascade dispatch, recharge and equipment-state transitions. Public experiments can support tank pressure/thermal and flow submodel checks. Public consequence datasets can support release, ignition, dispersion, detector and ventilation components.

## What is still missing

No independently attested local cohort currently contains the same event's vehicle or receptacle pressure, vehicle/tank temperature, delivered mass or SOC, dispenser flow and protocol/controller state on a common time base. The complete H70 station-to-vehicle external-validation gate therefore remains open.

Raw local rows, source paths, filenames, exact dates and site or manufacturer identifiers remain outside the repository. See the machine-readable [inventory](local_hrs_corpus_inventory_2026_10_09.json).
