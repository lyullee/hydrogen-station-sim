# Public raw-data search recheck (2026-10-10)

## Result

The final targeted check covered the two strongest public leads for an
external station-to-vehicle holdout:

- Kuroki et al. (2021), DOI
  [10.1016/j.ijhydene.2021.04.037](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2021.04.037)
- Wieser et al. (2025), DLR repository record and manuscript,
  [DLR record](https://elib.dlr.de/213625/) and
  [published manuscript](https://elib.dlr.de/213625/1/manuscript_final_published_version.pdf)

The records confirm that measurement data were used for model validation and
show useful channel roles. No public download was found that provides all of
the following together:

1. synchronized station/dispenser/receiving-vessel time series;
2. channel dictionary, units, and clock semantics; and
3. permission for independent re-scoring and redistribution of derived results.

The DLR material remains a figure-level measurement example. The Kuroki
publication remains an article-level actual-station validation claim. Neither
is admitted as a full-loop external holdout.

## Decision

Do not keep expanding web searches as if another article automatically solves
the data gap. The next evidence-producing step is a custodian-mediated,
privacy-safe request using the staged contract in
`BAM_KETI_DATA_REQUEST_DRAFT_2026_10_10.md`. If a custodian cannot release
raw rows, the current project still has a defensible station-side/component
validation scope; the full-loop claim must remain out of scope.

No raw rows, site identity, company/manufacturer identity, or calendar dates
were copied into the repository by this search.
