# Ekoto et al. (2012) mass-flow holdout result

The prospectively frozen, bounded transient mass-flow claim is **supported**.

- 39 unique Figure 3 experimental coordinates were eligible.
- Peak-normalized RMSE was 3.84% (required <=15%).
- Median absolute percentage error was 12.79% (required <=20%).
- Half-peak-time error was 6.07% (required <=20%).
- All three required screens passed without post-freeze changes.

Measured and predicted peak flow were 0.04483 and 0.05124 kg/s. Measured and
predicted half-peak time were 0.5064 and 0.5371 s. The result supports transfer
of the locked source-depletion model for this 13.45 MPa, 3.63 L scaled release.
It does not validate enclosure dispersion, ignition, overpressure, forklift
geometry, 70-90 MPa storage, station control or separation distance.
