# Draft data request for the HyDelta hydrogen overpumping experiments

**Do not send without the project owner's review.**

Subject: Request for de-identified hydrogen overpumping experiment traces

Dear HyDelta / Kiwa Technology and Výzkumný a zkušební ústav Plzeň team,

I am preparing an independent validation study of a virtual hydrogen-refuelling
station safety digital twin. The STARFOS result *Experimentally obtained
hydrogen overpumping data for validation of mathematical models* (RIV
code RIV/25870807:_____/23:N0000009) describes four hydrogen transfer
experiments with a high-pressure cylinder bundle, receiving cylinder, hose and
transfer valves. The report states that pressure, temperature and flow were
recorded at multiple locations, including inside/on the receiving cylinder and
downstream of the transfer valve.

Could you share a de-identified machine-readable export, or identify the
approved custodian or repository, for the four experiments? The minimum useful
fields are:

- synchronized elapsed time and sampling interval;
- source/bundle pressure, receiving-cylinder pressure and transfer flow;
- receiving-cylinder gas or wall temperature and downstream temperature;
- cylinder volume, geometry, initial pressure/temperature and gas inventory;
- valve/hose configuration, operating steps and any pressure-relief or stop
  markers;
- sensor calibration, uncertainty and quality flags.

CSV, XLSX or a controlled repository export is sufficient. Please state the
licence or written reuse terms, including whether derived error metrics,
figures and aggregated traces may be published in an open repository and a
journal article. We do not need operator or facility identifiers.

Any received files would be quarantined and cryptographically hashed. The
eligibility criteria, evaluation protocol and model commit would be frozen
before inspecting outcomes; plotted figures will not be digitised as a
substitute for the logger export.
