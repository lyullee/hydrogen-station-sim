# Prospective protocol: Zenodo 4106101 unignited pressure-peaking experiments

This protocol was frozen before the raw pressure, mass-flow, and concentration archives were downloaded or inspected. Only the public record metadata and file checksums were read before freezing.

- Source: [Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.4106101](https://zenodo.org/records/4106101)
- Related publication: DOI 10.1016/j.ijhydene.2020.08.221
- License: CC BY 4.0
- Scope: confined, vented, unignited hydrogen pressure-peaking consequence submodel

The primary metrics are per-case peak overpressure absolute error <=5 kPa and peak-time relative error <=20%. A confirmatory screen requires at least six eligible cases and at least 80% of cases passing both primary metrics. Cases may be excluded only for documented raw-data or metadata failure; no case selection or parameter fitting is allowed after the outcomes are seen.

This dataset cannot validate the complete HRS filling loop, outdoor dispersion, ignition, or emergency response. Raw archives remain under the ignored `data/public_validation/raw/` directory; only hashes, derived results, and the runner are committed.
