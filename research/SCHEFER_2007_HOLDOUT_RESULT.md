# Schefer et al. (2007) pressure-decay holdout result

The prospectively frozen pressure-decay claim is **not supported**.

- 222 unique Figure 4 coordinates were eligible.
- Initial-pressure-normalized RMSE was 11.58% (required <=10%).
- Median absolute percentage error was 27.46% (required <=15%).
- Half-pressure-time error was 6.51% (required <=20%).
- One of three required screens passed; the joint decision therefore failed.

The locked model reproduced the initial pressure and the measured half-pressure
time reasonably closely. It did not reproduce the complete curve: the measured
record shows a fast initial pressure loss, whereas the well-mixed model falls
more slowly at first and then underpredicts the late pressure. Valve/manifold
line-pack and heat exchange are plausible missing mechanisms, but this is a
post-outcome diagnosis. No parameter was changed and the primary result was not
rerun.

This result is bounded to the vessel pressure trace. It does not validate or
invalidate flame, radiation, dispersion, station control or separation distance.
