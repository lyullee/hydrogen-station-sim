# FCH2Rail IJHE measurement-access recheck

The open-access IJHE article (DOI `10.1016/j.ijhydene.2025.04.040`) confirms a
real FCH2Rail station-to-train measurement campaign. It describes vehicle tank
pressure and temperature plus dispenser pressure, temperature and mass flow,
and shows a common-time measurement figure.

The same article states on PDF page 2 that no measurement data for hydrogen
rail refuelling were publicly available. The DLR record exposes the article PDF
but no machine-readable logger archive, channel dictionary, calibration file or
written terms for publishing derived metrics. The source is therefore useful
for topology and channel face-validity, and as a high-value data-request lead,
but it is not an untouched numerical holdout.

The raw-data request must be completed and rights recorded before the dataset
can enter the full-loop protocol. No numerical result is promoted from this
access recheck.
