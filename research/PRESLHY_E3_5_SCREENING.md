# PRESLHY E3.5 public-data screening

- Dataset: *Fuel Cells and Hydrogen Joint Undertaking (FCH JU); Summary of experiment series E3.5*.
- DOI: [10.35097/1481](https://doi.org/10.35097/1481).
- Repository: [RADAR/KIT record](https://www.radar-service.eu/radar/en/dataset/nWczysTWjmuzgFKm).
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Archive: 11.3 GB tar archive, MD5 `aa89132abb34eff9b2b4ce40a6e2975f`.

The record describes 25 elevated liquid-hydrogen releases through 6, 12 and 25.4 mm nozzles at indicated tanker pressures of 1 or 5 barg. The archive contains pressure, tank pressure, mass-flow, near/far thermocouples, hydrogen concentration, H2 PPM, oxygen depletion, weather/wind/humidity and video channels. The sensor set is valuable independent evidence for detector logic, concentration-field rendering, release-source reconstruction and visual accident replay.

It is deliberately excluded from the primary gaseous high-pressure HRS fueling-loop gate: the phase, release geometry and pressure domain differ from the station model, and the record does not provide vehicle fill, cascade, compressor, precooler or dispenser-controller traces. It must therefore be cited only for consequence/detector/visualisation validation unless a separate, pre-registered liquid-release model is added.
