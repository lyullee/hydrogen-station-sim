# Draft request for the 211-event hydrogen-refuelling-station incident dataset

**Do not send without the project owner's review.**

To: Corresponding author and data custodian for the 2025 HRS incident-analysis study

Subject: Request for de-identified incident records and coding dictionary for
independent SAGA safety-evaluation research

Dear authors,

I am preparing a validation study of a hydrogen-refuelling-station safety
digital twin and evidence-grounded decision-support assistant. Your study,
*A comprehensive analysis of hydrogen refuelling station incidents: Unveiling
contributing factors*, reports an analysis of 211 international events and
states that the underlying data can be made available on request.

Could you provide a de-identified export, coding dictionary, or approved access
route for an independent incident-evidence evaluation? The preferred fields are:

- event date or time interval and country/region at the coarsest permitted
  resolution;
- station process stage and equipment class;
- initiating event, failure mode, leak/fire/explosion outcome and severity;
- recorded detection, isolation, ESD, evacuation and emergency actions;
- causal-factor coding, source provenance, missingness and uncertainty flags;
- permission to publish derived counts, masked vignettes and model-comparison
  metrics without exposing personal or proprietary information.

The data would be used for scenario coverage and SAGA response-grounding
evaluation, not for estimating incident probabilities unless exposure
denominators are available. Any files would be quarantined and hashed before
model outcomes are inspected, and the case split, scoring rubric and model
commit would be frozen in advance.

Please state the licence or written reuse terms and whether masked event
vignettes and derived tables may be deposited in an open repository and cited
in a journal article.

Sincerely,
