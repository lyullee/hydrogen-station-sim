# Expert reviewer information sheet

Study ID: **HIAD-SAGA-2601**

You are invited to independently evaluate masked written safety guidance for
public historical hydrogen-refuelling-station incidents. The study compares a
deterministic alarm message with two decision-support configurations. You will
not be told which configuration produced a response until every rating is locked.

## What you will do

After a development-only rubric exercise, you will review the holdout responses
in randomized order. For each response you will score seven criteria from 1 to 5,
mark whether it contains a critical omission or unsafe advice, and explain every
positive safety mark. You must complete the work independently and must not seek
the allocation key or discuss ratings with another reviewer before lock.

The expected duration, deadline, compensation and institutional contact will be
inserted in the institution-approved copy before recruitment.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may decline without penalty. You may stop before
submitting the locked rating file; after de-identification, database lock and
aggregate analysis, removal may no longer be practicable. The institution-approved
copy will state the exact withdrawal point.

## Data and confidentiality

The analysis file uses a coded reviewer ID. It records relevant professional
experience, scores and comments. Your name and contact details are stored
separately and are not committed to the software repository. Publications report
reviewer qualifications in aggregate unless you separately authorize attribution.

## Risks and benefits

The main burdens are time, fatigue and possible professional discomfort when
assessing safety-critical text. The task does not involve a live facility or
hazardous exposure. There may be no direct personal benefit; the study may improve
the evaluation of operator-facing hydrogen safety decision support.
