# KIT large-scale LH2 consequence staged-intake protocol

The official KIT RADAR record `10.35097/1483` describes a 5 GB CC BY-SA 4.0
archive with pressure, mass-flow, blast-pressure, heat-flux, pipework
temperature, weather and video evidence from large-scale liquid-hydrogen cloud
release and combustion experiments. A repository-wide provenance search found
only earlier metadata notes; no local numerical result or raw archive was found.

This first stage is frozen before archive download. It permits checksum
verification, archive-member listing and reading of apparatus, geometry,
calibration, data-dictionary and experiment-index documents. It prohibits
opening signal values, viewing videos or outcome images, selecting cases by
their effects, fitting parameters and committing raw data.

If the metadata establish usable cases and channels, a second protocol must
freeze the model domain, all-eligible-case rule, mappings, units, code hashes,
error endpoints and uncertainty treatment before any signals are opened. This
separation matters because delayed ignition of a congested LH2 cloud may lie
outside the current gaseous jet-fire model. Stage 1 supports intake eligibility
only and cannot validate HyRAM, safety distances, station control or SAGA.
