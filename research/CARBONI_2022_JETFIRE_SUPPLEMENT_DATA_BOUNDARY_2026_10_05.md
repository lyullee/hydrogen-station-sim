# Carboni et al. (2022) jet-fire supplement: data-boundary record

The IJHE article [*Experimental and numerical investigation of horizontal
hydrogen jet fires*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2022.05.010) states that
the complete measurements are supplied as supplementary material. The
publisher CDN exposes a DOCX supplement at
`https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0360319922019814-mmc1.docx`.

The artifact was inspected locally only to establish its scope and integrity:
it contains ANSYS Fluent settings, an experimental/calculated flame-length and
heat-power table, and plots/embedded figures for 17 horizontal-release tests
covering roughly 90--450 bar and 1--5 mm nozzles. Its SHA-256 is recorded in
the companion JSON. The raw DOCX and its figures are not redistributed here.

This is **not** promoted to a validation dataset. The article is not marked
open access and no reuse licence for the supplement was established. The
public artifact is also plot/figure based rather than a rights-cleared,
machine-readable synchronized station/vehicle log. It therefore cannot
support a new repository score, model tuning, or a full-loop HRS claim.

The appropriate next step is a rights-cleared author/publisher request for
machine-readable derived values or the underlying measurements, followed by a
pre-access frozen model and scoring protocol. Until then this record is only
traceability and a data-request lead; it does not alter the IJHE readiness or
goal-completion gates.

Machine-readable record: `research/carboni_2022_jetfire_supplement_data_boundary_2026_10_05.json`.
