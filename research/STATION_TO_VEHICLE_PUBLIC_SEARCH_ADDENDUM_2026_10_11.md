# Station-to-vehicle public search addendum (2026-10-11)

This addendum records newly indexed public leads checked after the frozen scope decision. A lead is not promoted to a validation dataset unless it provides row-level, machine-readable station/dispenser and vehicle/receptacle channels on a common clock, units/protocol metadata, and reuse rights.

| Lead | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caponi et al., 3Emotion operational data, DOI `10.1051/e3sconf/202233406008` | Aggregate context only | Operator-log summaries are reported, but no reusable synchronized raw trace was identified. |
| Kim et al., HRS safety anomaly platform, DOI `10.3390/app16157856` | Article/context only | The article describes field monitoring; its public material does not provide a rights-cleared station-to-vehicle raw holdout. |
| IPCEI European HRS inventory | Registry only | Station and dispenser context is available without vehicle-tank transient channels. |

The primary scope and the paper limitation are unchanged.
