# KIT hydrogen-release consequence-data reuse note

The KIT Open Data record [10.35097/1483](https://doi.org/10.35097/1483)
describes a public 5 GB experimental archive from a hydrogen release/dispersion
campaign. Its metadata lists blast-pressure, noise, heat-flux, pipework
thermocouple, pressure, mass-flow, weather and video channels, and the record
states a CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

This is a real consequence-experiment candidate for the HRS safety twin's
release, detector and impact submodels. It is not a vehicle-fueling trace and
cannot close the station controller/full-loop gate. Before use, retain the
record metadata and archive digest, confirm the licence covers derived plots,
and freeze a consequence-only protocol before opening case outcomes. The
archive should be handled through the external intake contract; no values are
treated as inspected by this note.
