# HyDelta D6A.1 indoor spatial holdout protocol

This protocol freezes the exact H2SAFE orientation-class development candidate
before the HyDelta D6A.1 report file is downloaded or opened. Search-index
metadata had already revealed the presence of 50 movable gas sensors, the
50--1000 normal dm3/h release range, 10/20/30 m3 container arrangements and
qualitative ventilation findings. No per-sensor numerical outcome or report
figure had been inspected at freeze time.

The holdout is eligible only if the report supplies at least four hydrogen
experiments, at least ten sensor coordinates in the same frame as the source,
a declared release-orientation class and tabulated per-sensor numerical
responses. Figures will not be digitized to rescue eligibility. The four fixed
rank/recall screens are recorded in the JSON companion. Every eligible case and
every failure remains in the result.

A pass would support only indoor spatial-ranking transfer for the reported
actual-hydrogen configurations. It cannot calibrate station detector amplitude
or thresholds and cannot validate outdoor dispersion, HRS detector placement,
CFD, ignition, ESD, consequence distance or a complete station loop.

Source: [HyDelta D6A.1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8154318](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8154318).
