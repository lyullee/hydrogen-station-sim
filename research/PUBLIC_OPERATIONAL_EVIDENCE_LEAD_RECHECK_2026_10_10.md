# Public operational evidence lead recheck (2026-10-10)

Two official sources were rechecked because they can strengthen the twin without pretending that aggregate evidence is a synchronized full-loop validation set.

- The [CARB 2024 in-use HRS study](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/Existing%20Light-Duty%20Hydrogen%20Refueling%20Stations%20In-Use%20Study%20Report%20ADA%20AL.pdf) reports HyStEP testing at 22 operating stations and provides protocol, fault-test and example fueling-result context. The public report does not expose a rights-cleared common-clock logger archive, so it remains an aggregate field benchmark.
- The [NIST Transient Flow Facility](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/transient-flow-facility) documents dispenser-like pressure, temperature and flow transients and metrology targets. The public project page does not publish a reusable station-to-vehicle logger cohort, so it remains component and instrumentation context.

Both sources are useful for controller coverage, operating-envelope plausibility and grounded LLM explanations. Neither closes the independent station-to-vehicle holdout. The smallest next request remains three de-identified events with common elapsed time, station/dispenser pressure, boundary temperature, mass flow or transferred mass and protocol phase.

No private source path, site identity, manufacturer, calendar date or raw measurement row was added.
