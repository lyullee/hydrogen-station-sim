# Draft request for the 250 bar ferry Type-IV filling experiments

**Review the sender identity and institutional route before sending.**

Subject: Request for de-identified 250 bar Type-IV ferry-tank filling traces

Dear authors and data custodian,

I am preparing an independent validation study of a hydrogen-refuelling-station
safety digital twin. Your article, *Developing Science-based fueling protocols
for 250-bar hydrogen tanks onboard hydrogen ferries: Experiments and modeling*
(DOI <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2026.153768>), reports controlled fills
of a large Type-IV tank with a nominal hydrogen capacity of about 28 kg. The
experiment is valuable for testing geometry and capacity transfer beyond
light-duty vehicle tanks.

Could you provide the machine-readable experimental traces or an approved
custodian route? The minimum useful fields are:

- common elapsed time and sampling interval;
- source, dispenser and tank pressure;
- inlet mass flow or cumulative transferred mass;
- inlet-gas, internal thermocouple-tree, wall and ambient temperatures;
- tank internal volume and geometry, injector geometry and thermocouple
  locations;
- initial pressure, temperature and mass/SOC;
- precooling set point, control schedule, stop/abort reason and final SOC; and
- calibration, uncertainty, units and quality flags.

Because the paper reports aggregate SOFIL accuracy, an unreported repeat or a
custodian-held blind split is preferred. The blind case identifiers and
outcomes should remain undisclosed until we provide a timestamped model commit,
eligibility criteria and scoring protocol. If only the published cases can be
shared, we will treat them as post-publication geometry-transfer diagnostics,
not an untouched H70 station validation.

Please state whether de-identified data, derived metrics and figures may be
used in a journal article and deposited in an open repository. Site, vessel
operator and equipment-manufacturer identities are not required.

Sincerely,

*[name, affiliation, institutional email and project DOI to be supplied]*
