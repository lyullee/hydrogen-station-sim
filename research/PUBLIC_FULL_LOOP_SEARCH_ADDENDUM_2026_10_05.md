# Public full-loop search addendum (2026-10-05)

Twelve additional leads were checked after the H2Protocol case inventory was
closed.  They cover storage-tank, confined-release and pressure-reduction
experiments, static station inventories, real-fill and multi-station reports,
the NREL H2FillS simulator, an open metal-hydride compressor dataset, the
MetHyTrucks HySaM sampling workbooks, and the FCH2RAIL railway refueling
measurements.  None provides the required synchronized station-to-vehicle raw
archive with pressure, mass/flow, a common time base and publication reuse
terms.

The addendum is an exclusion record only.  It does not convert paper plots,
simulator outputs, aggregate operations tables, or component experiments into
an HRS full-loop holdout.  The two open component datasets may be considered
for separately preregistered compressor/instrument sanity checks, but cannot
close the full-loop gate.
See the accompanying JSON for URLs, license observations and the eligibility
decision for each candidate.
