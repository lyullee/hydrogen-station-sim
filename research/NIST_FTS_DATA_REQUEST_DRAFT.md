# Draft data request to NIST Hydrogen Field Test Standard custodians

**Do not send without the project owner's review.**

To: NIST Fluid Metrology Group / corresponding author of DOI
10.1016/j.flowmeasinst.2015.10.010

Subject: Request for de-identified Hydrogen Field Test Standard logger traces

Dear NIST Fluid Metrology Group,

I am preparing an independent validation study of a hydrogen-refuelling-station
safety digital twin. Your paper, *Hydrogen Field Test Standard: Laboratory and
Field Performance* (DOI 10.1016/j.flowmeasinst.2015.10.010), reports a 35 MPa
Type III field-test standard used at a retail hydrogen dispenser. The paper
describes three 0.41 kg and four 0.75 kg H₂ field drafts, continuous tank
pressure and temperature measurements, and independent gravimetric, PVT and
master-meter mass determinations.

Could you provide de-identified logger files or an approved access route for
these tests? The minimum useful fields are:

- synchronized elapsed time and sampling interval;
- test-standard tank pressure and each temperature channel;
- master-meter instantaneous or cumulative mass and gravimetric/PVT start and
  end masses;
- dispenser inlet/source pressure and temperature, if retained;
- draft identifier, target mass, initial conditions, ambient conditions and
  vent/blow-down markers;
- sensor calibration history, uncertainty budget and quality flags.

The dispenser readout can remain excluded; the data can be used as an
independent metrology/partial-dispenser holdout rather than a claim about the
station controller. Please state the licence or written reuse and citation
terms, including whether derived error metrics and figures may be published in
an open repository and journal article.

Any received files would be quarantined and cryptographically hashed before
model outcomes are inspected. Eligibility criteria, the evaluation protocol
and the model commit would be frozen before evaluation.

Sincerely,
