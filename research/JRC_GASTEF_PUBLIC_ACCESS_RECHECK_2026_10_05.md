# JRC GasTeF public-access recheck (2026-10-05)

The official JRC record [JRC76380](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC76380) describes the GasTeF onboard-tank reference-data work and states that experimental data are intended for public reference. The current record view was inspected directly. Its `Datasets` and `Dataset collections` sections contain no public URL, and the record exposes no machine-readable pressure/temperature/time files or raw-trace reuse terms.

The record is therefore retained as a controlled-access lead, not as an external validation holdout. The associated open-access IJHE article is [10.1016/j.ijhydene.2014.03.227](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2014.03.227), but article-level CC BY status does not by itself establish permission to redistribute or numerically reuse unpublished raw traces.

- Decision: `CONTROLLED_ACCESS_LEAD_NO_PUBLIC_RAW_ARCHIVE`
- Full-loop holdout eligible: **false**
- Machine-readable raw trace retrieved: **false**
- Reproduction record: `research/jrc_gastef_public_access_recheck_2026_10_05.json`
