# Draft data request to CARB

**Do not send without the project owner's review.**

To: CARB Vapor Recovery and Fuel Transfer Branch (`vapor@arb.ca.gov`)

Subject: Request for de-identified fill-level data from the 2024 hydrogen station in-use study

Dear CARB Vapor Recovery and Fuel Transfer Branch,

I am preparing an academic validation study of a research and training digital
twin for hydrogen refuelling-station safety. I would like to request the
de-identified test workbook and, where releasable, the fill-level time-series
data underlying the *Existing Light-Duty Hydrogen Refueling Stations In-Use
Study Report* dated 22 November 2024.

Appendix A states that the study results were tabulated in an Excel workbook,
and the report figures show pressure, temperature, mass-flow and SOC traces. For
reproducible validation, the most useful fields would be:

- elapsed time or timestamp and units;
- station/dispenser and CHSS/receptacle pressure;
- station, fuel and CHSS/receptacle temperature;
- instantaneous mass flow or cumulative delivered mass;
- SOC if available;
- CHSS capacity category or volume, initial state, communication mode, fueling
  protocol/version and temperature category;
- test quality, exclusion or instrument-status flags.

Station and operator identities are not required. A de-identified export is
sufficient. Please also advise the applicable licence or written reuse and
citation terms, including whether derived error metrics and figures may be
published in an open research repository and journal article.

The files would be quarantined and hashed before analysis. Case eligibility and
the model version would then be frozen before outcomes are inspected, and all
eligible failures would be retained. The software is a research prototype and
the study will not characterize the data as regulatory certification.

If the fill-level traces cannot be released, the Appendix A workbook alone, or
guidance to the correct data custodian, would still be valuable.

Sincerely,

*[name, affiliation, institutional email and project DOI to be supplied]*
