# Liang et al. (2026) high-pressure hydrogen release Table 2

`liang_2026_table2.csv` transcribes the seven numerical rows in Table 2 of:

> Liang Liyou et al., “Experimental research on high-pressure hydrogen leakage
> from storage tanks,” *Acta Energiae Solaris Sinica*, 47(4), 2026,
> pp. 593–598. DOI: https://doi.org/10.19912/j.0254-0096.tynxb.2024-2238

Official article page:
https://www.tynxb.org.cn/CN/10.19912/j.0254-0096.tynxb.2024-2238

The experiment used hydrogen, a 1 mm circular nozzle, a 55 L tank, a 3 m
release pipe with 11 mm internal diameter, and a horizontal atmospheric
release. `nozzle_pressure_mpa` is the reported pressure `p2` measured near the
nozzle. The paper standardizes the theoretical-flow calculation at 27 °C; the
benchmark therefore uses 300.15 K and 101325 Pa downstream pressure.

The CSV contains only the small factual numerical table required for
reproducibility. The publisher PDF and article figures are not redistributed.
The article's copyright remains with its rights holder. This transcription is
not a new license grant for the source article.

The numerical outcomes were inspected before this benchmark protocol was
written. The resulting comparison is a post-access, zero-fit diagnostic and
must not be described as a prospective holdout or independent validation.
